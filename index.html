Remembering Tim▼

What is Xanis1?

So you’ve seen the tattoo and you want to know more! Xanis1 was the handle of my friend, Tim, who passed away in early 2025.

Who was Xanis1 / Tim?

Tim was an avid RPG player, technology enthusiast, dreamer, philosopher in his own way, accomplished internet troll, and one of my oldest and dearest friends. He loved Final Fantasy and exploring new technologies.

Okay so now what?

Now Tim’s memory is kept alive with you as well. It was a simple thing: You saw something, you wanted to know more, and now the knowledge of Tim’s existence is stored somewhere in your brain for a time or maybe even forever.

Okay so what can I do?

Play an RPG, subscribe to an MMO, troll on social media, reflect on the fragility and shortness of our time here. Learn about the prevention and treatment of Sepsis, maybe consider donating blood?

In memory of Tim.
